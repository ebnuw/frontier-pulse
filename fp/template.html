Weekend Gap

US stocks stop trading Friday 16:00 ET, but Entropy's perps keep going. Just before Monday's open, the perp price is effectively a weekend {{t:predicted|prediction}}. We compare it with the {{t:actual|actual move}} to Monday's first regular-session price. Moves under 0.1% count as flat and are not scored. See {{t:weekend_gap|Weekend Gap}}.
