Weekend Gap

US stocks stop trading Friday 16:00 ET, but Entropy's perps keep trading. Just before Monday's open (09:15–09:30 ET) the perp price is effectively a weekend "prediction". We compare it with the first regular-session print (09:30–09:45 ET close) and with Friday's close. "Captured" is predicted ÷ actual move when both point the same way. Moves under 0.1% count as flat and are not scored for direction.
